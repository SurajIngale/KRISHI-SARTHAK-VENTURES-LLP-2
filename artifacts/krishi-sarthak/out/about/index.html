Company Overview

Who We Are

KRISHI SARTHAK VENTURES LLP is a registered Indian export-import trade firm with deep roots in agricultural sourcing and a forward-looking approach to global commerce. Incorporated under the Limited Liability Partnership Act, we operate with the agility of a boutique trade house and the compliance rigour of a large export enterprise.

Our name encapsulates our purpose — "Krishi" meaning agriculture and "Sarthak" meaning purposeful or meaningful. Together, we stand for purposeful agriculture commerce — trade that benefits farmers, buyers, and the global food supply chain.

We specialise in sourcing, processing, grading, and exporting a diverse portfolio of agricultural commodities including fresh produce, frozen vegetables, dehydrated powders, spices and herbs, pulses and grains, dry fruits and nuts, and select industrial materials such as gypsum, bitumen, and fly ash.

Beyond commodities, we offer comprehensive trade support services: freight forwarding, global trade consultancy, documentation and licensing assistance — making us a true one-stop partner for international buyers seeking reliable Indian agricultural exports.

How We Work

Our Trade Approach

A disciplined 4-stage process that transforms agricultural surplus into verified, export-ready shipments.

01

Market Intelligence

We analyse real-time demand signals, seasonal cycles, and international buyer requirements before committing to any sourcing plan.

02

Verified Sourcing

Our on-ground teams identify and audit agricultural clusters, cooperatives, and processing units to ensure origin-authenticity and crop quality.
