Quality & Processing Standards

Commercial Integrity Across Every Consignment

KRISHI SARTHAK VENTURES LLP adheres to disciplined quality sorting, count uniformity, moisture-tight barrier packaging, and international export documentation protocols.

Strict Size & Count Grading

Standardized whole kernel count grading (such as Cashew W180/W240/W320), Mamra almond purity inspection, and Sortex defect elimination.

Moisture & Freshness Control

Controlled dehydration standards for raisins and walnuts, preventing rancidity and protecting natural crunch, texture, and aroma.

Vacuum & Nitrogen-Flushing

Multi-layer vacuum barrier pouches, nitrogen-flushed tins, and export-grade heavy-duty corrugated master cartons engineered for multi-week ocean freight.

Comprehensive Export Papers

End-to-end documentation coordination including Phytosanitary Certificates, Certificate of Origin, Bill of Lading, and international laboratory analysis.
