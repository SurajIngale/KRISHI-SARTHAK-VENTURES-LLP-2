About the company

Your trusted partner in international agricultural trade

KRISHI SARTHAK VENTURES LLP is an export-oriented enterprise committed to delivering premium-grade agricultural commodities, fresh fruits, vegetables, frozen produce, and industrial minerals to international markets.

We bridge domestic farm clusters with global importers through transparent sourcing, rigorous quality grading, cold-chain integrity, and end-to-end trade coordination.
