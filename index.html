About the company

Your partner in global trade

KRISHI SARTHAK VENTURES LLP is focused on connecting businesses with international markets through agricultural product sourcing, market intelligence and global trade support.

Our business approach emphasizes product quality, reliable sourcing, buyer–supplier relationships and efficient coordination throughout the import-export process.

We aim to help businesses identify market opportunities, understand international trade requirements and establish dependable commercial relationships across borders.

Our services cover product sourcing, international trade coordination, shipment support and documentation guidance. We strive to deliver professional service and help clients navigate the complexities of international trade.
